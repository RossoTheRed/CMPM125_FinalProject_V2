## Concept
**Theme:** Force Of Habit
<br>
**Genre:** 3D (Survival?), Horror, Puzzle
<br>
### **Pitch:**
 "*You* are a company engineer who has been sent down to repair a remote abyssal research station nestled deep within the Kermadec Trench. The last report recieved before communication ceased was that of an infestation; apparently, some yet discovered species of Amphipod has burrowed through the hull, seemingly taking a liking to the station's interior. It is presumed that the autonomous maintenance units (AMUs) are in some way indisposed, and likely now require manual on-site repair. Your job is to restore crucial systems,  such as the station's thermal power generator and numerous pieces of research equipment, as well as recover and re-program the AMUs (or possibly construct new ones, should they be too badly damaged.) it is believed that the station has roughly one week of emergency power left, after which the station will shut down and become unrecoverable. Should power be restored to working order, the station will be able to run a decontamination cycle that will eliminate all organic material present within. Management has reminded you that your transport shuttle will not decouple from the station airlock until it is confirmed that its systems are in working order, or a sufficiently significant impediment to your repairs has been identified and confirmed topside. Additionally, should the station lose power, so too will its airlock mechanism, preventing the shuttle from undocking. Good Luck..."
